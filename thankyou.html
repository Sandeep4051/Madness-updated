Thank You Notes

Tell us what they ordered and we will write them a short note. Every note comes out different.

1 Who is it for?

First name is enough. Leave it blank and we will just say "friend".

2 Which thickshake did they order?

The note is written around this, so pick the one in their hand.

3 Any occasion?

Only if there is one. A plain order works perfectly well.

4 Anything personal to add?

Optional. One short line from you, added to the end of the note.
